CITY OF PROVIDENCE 
INVITATION FOR BIDS (IFB) 
Parking Signs, Parking Sign Posts, and Related Parking Administration Materials – One-Year 
Contract with Two One-Year Renewal Options 
 
1. Purpose 
The City of Providence Department of Public Works, Parking Administration Division, seeks competitive bids 
from qualified manufacturers and distributors for the furnishing and delivery of parking regulatory signs, parking 
sign posts, mounting hardware, parking control devices, and related materials on an as-needed basis under a firm 
fixed unit price requirements contract. 
 
This contract is intended to provide a readily available source for replacement, maintenance, and installation of 
parking control devices throughout the City. 
 
2. Definitions 
For purposes of this solicitation, the following definitions shall apply: 
 
Business Day means Monday through Friday, excluding City-recognized holidays. 
 
City means the City of Providence, Rhode Island. 
 
Contractor means the successful bidder awarded a contract under this solicitation. 
 
Custom Sign means any parking sign or parking control device manufactured using City-provided artwork, text, 
dimensions, or other specifications that differ from standard catalog products. 
 
Emergency Order means an order designated by the City requiring expedited production and delivery to address 
an immediate operational, public safety, weather-related, or other urgent need. 
 
Equivalent Product means a product that the Contractor demonstrates, to the satisfaction of the City, is equal or 
superior in quality, durability, performance, warranty, and compliance with applicable standards. 
 
Parking Administration Materials include parking signs, sign posts, mounting hardware, delineators, bollards, 
barricades, parking cones, parking control devices, and other related materials identified in this solicitation. 
 
Standard Sign means a commercially available parking sign manufactured in accordance with the current Manual 
on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD). 
 
Stock Item means a standard catalog item maintained in the Contractor's inventory and available for immediate 
shipment. 
 
3. Contract Type 
 
This solicitation will result in a Firm Fixed Unit Price, Blanket Requirements, Estimated Quantity Contract. 
 
A. Firm Fixed Unit Price Contract 
The Contractor shall provide all awarded items at the fixed unit prices submitted in its bid for the duration of the 
initial contract term. Unit prices shall include all labor, materials, manufacturing, packaging, freight, delivery, 
overhead, profit, and all other costs necessary to furnish the requested items. Except as otherwise provided for 
approved renewal-term price adjustments, unit prices shall remain firm throughout the contract term.

B. Blanket Requirements Contract 
This is a requirements-type contract established to provide the City with a readily available source for parking 
signs, posts, hardware, and related materials on an as-needed basis. Individual purchases will be made through the 
issuance of purchase orders during the contract term. The City may place orders at any time and in varying 
quantities based on operational needs. 
 
C. Estimated Quantity Contract 
The quantities identified in this solicitation are estimates provided solely for the purpose of bid evaluation and 
contract planning. They are based on historical usage and anticipated future demand and are not guaranteed. The 
City reserves the right to purchase greater or lesser quantities than those estimated, or to omit the purchase of any 
item entirely, depending on funding availability, operational requirements, and the City's best interests. The 
Contractor shall furnish any quantities ordered at the awarded unit prices during the contract term. 
 
The City does not guarantee any minimum purchase quantity. 
 
Estimated quantities are provided solely for bid evaluation. 
 
4. Contract Term 
 
One-year base term with two optional one-year renewals, subject to satisfactory performance, funding availability, 
and mutual agreement. Renewal price adjustments may be requested only during renewal periods and shall not 
exceed the lesser of the annual CPI-U increase or three percent, supported by documentation. 
 
5. Contractor Qualifications  
 
Bidder shall have at least five years of experience manufacturing traffic control signs and provide three municipal 
references demonstrating comparable work. 
 
6. Shareable Contract 
 
At the City's discretion, other governmental, quasi-governmental, educational, and public agencies may purchase 
from the resulting contract under the same terms and conditions. 
 
7. Scope of Work 
The Contractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, materials, manufacturing, fabrication, quality control, 
packaging, equipment, transportation, delivery, warranty administration, technical support, and all other resources 
necessary to provide parking regulatory signs, sign posts, mounting hardware, parking control devices, and related 
parking administration materials on an as-needed basis. 
 
Products shall include, but are not be limited to, standard and custom parking regulatory signs, ADA parking signs, 
resident permit signs, loading zone signs, tow-away signs, street cleaning signs, snow emergency signs, temporary 
parking restriction signs, fold-down reserved parking signs, parking meter signs, event parking signs, sign blanks, 
parking curb signs, flexible delineators, bollards, barricades, parking cones, sign posts, mounting hardware, and 
related accessories. 
 
The Contractor shall manufacture, inspect, package, and deliver all products in accordance with the requirements of 
this solicitation and applicable industry standards. 
 
The Contractor shall not substitute products, materials, reflective sheeting, hardware, or manufacturers without the 
prior written approval of the City. 
 
Equivalent products may be proposed provided the Contractor submits sufficient documentation demonstrating 
compliance with all applicable specifications. The City shall be the sole judge of equivalency.

8. Optional Add/Alternate Sign Installation Services 
The City may, at its sole discretion, award the installation portion of this solicitation to the same contractor awarded 
the materials contract or may elect not to award this portion at all. 
 
Pricing for installation services shall be evaluated separately from the base bid and shall not affect the 
determination of the lowest responsive and responsible bidder for the materials portion of the solicitation unless 
otherwise stated in the Bid Form. 
 
The Contractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, vehicles, traffic control, equipment, tools, excavation 
equipment, concrete, disposal, restoration, permits (if required), utility coordination, and incidentals necessary to 
install, remove, replace, relocate, or repair parking signs, sign posts, and related parking control devices throughout 
the City. 
 
Installation services may include, but are not limited to: 
 
• Installation of new parking signs. 
• Removal and replacement of existing parking signs. 
• Installation of U-channel posts. 
• Installation of square tube posts. 
• Installation of breakaway sign support systems. 
• Installation of anchor bases. 
• Installation of sign hardware. 
• Removal and lawful disposal of damaged or obsolete signs and posts. 
• Relocation of existing signs. 
• Emergency replacement following accidents, vandalism, weather events, or public safety incidents. 
• Installation of temporary parking restriction signs for construction, events, emergencies, or snow operations. 
 
The Contractor shall verify the installation location with the City's representative before beginning work. 
 
The Contractor shall comply with all applicable utility locating requirements, including contacting Dig Safe®, 
before any excavation. 
 
The Contractor shall restore disturbed pavement, concrete, sidewalks, grass, or landscaped areas to a condition 
equal to or better than existed before installation. 
 
The Contractor shall be responsible for all traffic control necessary to safely perform the work in accordance with 
the latest edition of the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD). 
 
Unless otherwise authorized, installation work shall be completed within: 
 
• Routine Work: Five (5) business days after receipt of a work order. 
• Priority Work: Forty-eight (48) hours. 
• Emergency Work: Twenty-four (24) hours. 
 
All installed signs shall be: 
 
• Plumb.

9. Standards  
 
All products shall comply with the latest editions of the MUTCD, FHWA requirements, ASTM D4956, ASTM 
B209, ASTM A653, ASTM A123 where applicable, Rhode Island DOT standards, and Buy America requirements 
when federally funded. 
 
Additional standards may be required: ASTM A653, ASTM F3125 (fasteners), AASHTO, and NEMA standards 
where electronic devices are included.  
 
10. Materials  
 
Signs shall be fabricated from 5052-H38 aluminum (0.080-inch minimum thickness unless otherwise specified), 
with rounded corners, standard mounting holes, UV-resistant inks, and a minimum 10-year outdoor service life. 
Pricing shall be provided for Type IV, Type XI, and Diamond Grade reflective sheeting. 
 
11. Sign Posts and Hardware 
 
Provide pricing for galvanized U-channel and square tube posts, powder-coated options, breakaway systems, 
anchor bases, brackets, tamper-resistant hardware, stainless steel hardware, washers, nuts, bolts, pole caps, and 
related accessories.

14. Hardware 
 
Bidders are encouraged to provide pricing options for breakaway hardware, anchor bolts, hex bolts, tamper-
resistant bolts, nuts, washers, sign brackets, anti-rotation brackets, twin sign brackets, cross brackets, pole caps.  
 
Additional pricing for parking administration devices is also optional and may include, but is not limited to flexible 
delineators, parking curb signs, parking bollards, parking cones, fold-down reserved parking signs, portable no-
parking barricades, parking reservation signs, temporary event parking signs, and snow emergency parking signs 
 
15. Delivery  
 
Standard stock items shall be delivered within five business days. Custom signs shall be delivered within ten 
business days. Emergency orders shall be delivered within twenty-four hours whenever practicable. During 
declared emergencies, the Contractor shall prioritize City orders. 
 
16. Custom Signs 
 
The City will provide artwork or wording. The Contractor shall prepare electronic proofs in PDF format within 48 
hours and shall not begin production until written City approval. No artwork fee shall be charged. Artwork 
developed specifically for the City shall become the property of the City. 
 
17. Inspection and Acceptance 
 
The City reserves the right to inspect all products upon delivery. Defective or non-conforming materials shall be 
replaced within five business days at no additional cost. 
 
18. Warranty  
 
Provide a minimum ten-year manufacturer's warranty covering reflectivity, delamination, peeling, cracking, 
corrosion, fading, adhesive failure, ink failure, and manufacturing defects. 
 
19. Sustainability  
 
Whenever economically feasible, products should incorporate recycled materials and environmentally responsible 
manufacturing and packaging. 
 
20. Performance Standards 
 
Maintain at least 99% order accuracy; proofs within 48 hours; emergency response within 24 hours; replacement of 
defective products within five business days. 
 
21. Performance Standards for Installation 
 
The Contractor shall maintain the following minimum performance standards for installation services:

22. Reporting  
 
Upon request, provide annual usage reports, sales reports, product discontinuation notices, updated catalogs, and

In case of product discontinuation, the Contractor will notify the City within 30 days, recommend replacements,

23. Evaluation  
 
Award shall be made to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder based on the aggregate evaluated price and 
the bidder's demonstrated responsibility, delivery capability, product quality, warranty, references, and compliance 
with the specifications. The City reserves the right to request clarifications, product literature, samples, and 
additional information prior to award, waive minor informalities, reject any or all bids, and determine the bid most 
advantageous to the City. 
 
Base Bid: Award for the furnishing of parking signs, posts, and related materials shall be made to the lowest 
responsive and responsible bidder. 
 
Optional Installation Services: The City reserves the right to award the optional installation services to the same 
contractor, to a different contractor through a separate procurement, or not to award the installation portion at all, if 
determined to be in the City's best interest.
