How Do You Apply for a Team? 
 
Applications are handled by Assistant 
Program Directors (APDs). To apply: 
• Determine the project dates when 
you’d like to receive a team. 
• Contact us by emailing 
NCCCProjectApplications@AmeriCorps.gov. 
• Follow the instructions to submit a 
complete application by the due date. 
• Make your application stand out!  
(See page 2.) 
• We will notify applicants of award 
approximately six weeks prior to 
the project start date. 
What Do Our Teams Do? 
 
Project focus areas include:   
• Natural and other disasters 
• Infrastructure improvement 
• Environmental stewardship 
• Energy conservation 
• Urban and rural 
development 
Who Can Receive a Team? 
 
Qualifying organizations include: 
• Nonprofit and faith-based 
organizations 
• Government entities (federal, 
state, and local) 
• Public schools and universities 
• Native American tribal councils 
AmeriCorps NCCC is a federal, national service program for 18- to 24-
year-olds that strengthens communities and develops leaders through 
team-based national and community service. We partner with 
organizations like yours across the country to meet community-identified 
needs while developing our Volunteers personally and professionally.

Make Your Application Stand Out! 
 
If your proposed project involves any of the 
following elements, please highlight them in 
your application: 
 
• Social media posts specifically tagging 
@AmeriCorpsNCCC, featuring the team’s 
contribution to your project and the 
benefit to your community 
• High community exposure for the team 
(e.g., media opportunities, interaction with 
public officials, large events) 
• Service recipients who are veterans or 
military families 
• Disaster prevention, preparedness, 
recovery, or mitigation 
• Recruitment or coordination of other 
volunteers in the community 
Your Role in Developing Leaders 
 
We believe project partners are some of the 
best resources for our Volunteers’ professional 
and personal development. We ask you and 
your staff to support your team through: 
 
• Service-learning •  Coaching 
• Communication •  Feedback 
What Do Partner Organizations Provide? 
 
• At least 40 hours per week of enriching service 
that meets the needs named in your application 
• A broad range of different types of service 
experiences for the team 
• Daily technical supervision for the team, 
including on-the-job training 
• Orientation to the organization and community 
• All project materials, tools, and protective gear 
• Any work permits required to complete service 
• Service-learning education on community needs 
and deeper issues related to the project 
• Housing for the team, to include a full kitchen, 
full bathroom with showers, cleaning supplies, 
and toilet paper for the duration of the project 
Pronunciation: Ameri-Core N-triple-C 
  
 
 
What Does AmeriCorps NCCC Provide? 
 
• Team of 8-12 Volunteers with various 
backgrounds 
• Team Leader to coordinate the team (note: 
they are not a technical skills supervisor) 
• Van and fuel for team transportation 
• Food budget for the team to prepare meals 
• Basic Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), 
including hardhats, gloves, boots, and ear 
and eye protection 
• Coverage for the team through the Federal 
Tort Claims Act and Federal Employees’ 
Compensation Act 
• FBI criminal background checks for the team
